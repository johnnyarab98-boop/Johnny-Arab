A little about
the author.

Johnny Arab writes stories about the human things that linger: love, absence, ambition, tenderness, and the complicated spaces between what we say and what we mean. His work moves across worlds and genres, always returning to the quiet details that make a story unforgettable.

This is a place to discover his books, follow new releases, and step a little further into the worlds behind the pages.
